# Public-safe boundary

AI Product Motion Studies is an original product design and implementation artifact by Kevin Astuhuaman.

The scenarios, interface, data, motion states, and copy are synthetic. The repository does not contain Trackly production code, customer or applicant data, employer assets, confidential architecture, private prompts, credentials, or security-sensitive operating detail.

The examples communicate reusable product decisions: how motion can reveal structure, move authority, explain recovery, and preserve product truth across surfaces. They do not document a production authorization framework.
