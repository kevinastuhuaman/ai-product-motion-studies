# Decision 002: Motion needs a contract

## Context

Timing and choreography drift when they exist only in CSS or presentation software. Reviewers cannot distinguish deliberate continuity from an incidental transition.

## Decision

Publish the studies, states, timing, continuity graph, entry and exit objects, transition settings, and reduced-motion equivalent in **motion-spec.json**.

## Consequence

Implementation and product rationale can be reviewed separately, and automated checks can reject incomplete studies.
