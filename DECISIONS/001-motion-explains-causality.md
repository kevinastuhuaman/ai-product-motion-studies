# Decision 001: Motion explains causality

## Context

Product motion is often used to make an interface feel polished without improving comprehension. In AI workflows, that is dangerous because users already struggle to distinguish model activity, system state, authority, and outcome.

## Decision

Every transition in this artifact has one dominant intent: transform, sequence, or expand. Continuity objects remain stable so the viewer can identify the causal change. Decorative motion is excluded.

## Consequence

The sequence may feel quieter than a launch reel, but each state remains useful as a static frame and when reduced motion is enabled.
